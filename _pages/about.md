    <h1>Welcome!</h1>
    <p class="home-hero__lead">I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.hkbu.edu.hk/">Hong Kong Baptist University</a>, advised by <a href="https://guo-xiaoqing.github.io/">Prof. Xiaoqing Guo</a> in the <a href="https://ultravisionlab.github.io/">UltraVision+ Lab</a>.</p>
    <p>My research focuses on artificial intelligence for medical imaging, with a particular interest in 3D ultrasound reconstruction, neural rendering, and Gaussian Splatting.</p>
    <p>My current research focuses on 3D reconstruction from 2D ultrasound sequences. I am interested in using AI to build spatial-aware medical image representations.</p>
    <p>My long-term interest is to integrate medical image reconstruction, 3D representation learning, and human-AI interaction to support more efficient clinical workflows, such as helping clinicians understand scanned regions, assess spatial coverage, and improve the efficiency and reliability of clinical practice.</p>

<p class="home-background">Previously, I worked on accelerated MRI reconstruction and diffusion-based medical image reconstruction at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my B.Eng. degree in Biomedical Engineering from Shenzhen University.</p>
